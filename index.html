01 / THE CONTEXT

The Problem

Organic waste is often mixed with other waste.

This makes responsible treatment and reuse more difficult. When materials are not separated at the source, it becomes harder to understand which biological pathway may be appropriate.

BioCycle provides simple educational guidance on possible biological treatment and reuse pathways.
